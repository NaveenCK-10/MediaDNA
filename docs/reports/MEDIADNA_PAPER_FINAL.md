# MediaDNA: A Modular Framework for Multimodal Deepfake Forensics

**Abstract:** We present MediaDNA, a prototype forensic pipeline for analyzing synthetic media. By decoupling visual and acoustic representations into independent specialists and enforcing strict late fusion, we bypass representation collapse observed in monolithic architectures. Our diagnostic triad provides observational evidence consistent with multimodal contribution. While generalized accuracy remains a challenge, the platform demonstrates a rigorous, deployable architecture for transparent media analysis.
